Choose the session's current status from the allowed options. `waiting` means the work cannot continue until the user gives a specific answer, decision, approval, file or credential that the agent asked for (a pending question, an approval prompt, "which option do you want?"). A generic offer or closing line ("How can I help?", "Anything else?", "If you want, I can also…") is not waiting: choose `done` when the requested work is complete, otherwise `unfinished`. `unfinished` means work remains that the agent could continue on its own. `done` means the requested work is complete. Use the conversation excerpt, not the title alone.
